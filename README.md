# Clarity Prioritise

A quiet place to rank ideas. Three frameworks, no accounts, no tracking, no data leaving your browser.

## What it does

Clarity Prioritise helps product teams score and rank ideas using three established frameworks:

- **RICE**: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort. Calculates a single score so you can sort a backlog quickly.
- **Kano**: Groups items as Must-have, Performance, Delighter, Indifferent, or Reverse to surface what actually delights users.
- **Value vs Effort**: Plots items on a 2x2 quadrant so quick wins and time sinks become obvious.

You can run multiple boards, switch frameworks per board, export to JSON, and import boards from JSON.

## The privacy promise

Everything runs in your browser. There is no backend, no account system, no analytics, and no external requests. Your boards are saved to localStorage on your device. If you clear your browser data, your boards go with it. Use the Export feature to back them up.
